With numbers, \citet{knuth84} names the author before the number, while
\citep{plass81} and \cite{knuth84,plass81} give bracketed numbers only. A
page note \citep[p.~7]{knuth84} and a prefix \citep[see][]{plass81} follow
the same rules, and \citealp{hammer14} drops the brackets.
