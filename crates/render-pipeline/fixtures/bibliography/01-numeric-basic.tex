Line breaking follows Knuth and Plass \cite{plass81}, and the page builder
follows the \emph{Computers and Typesetting} series \cite{knuth84}. Both are
discussed together in several surveys \cite{knuth84,plass81} and at length in
the literature on incremental computation \cite{adapton}, which this note does
not repeat.

\bibitem{adapton} Matthew A. Hammer, Khoo Yit Phang, Michael Hicks and
  Jeffrey S. Foster. Adapton: composable, demand-driven incremental
