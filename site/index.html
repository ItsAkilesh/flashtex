LaTeX that typesets as fast as you think.

FlashTeX is a native Mac editor built on a LaTeX compiler written from scratch in Rust. The preview updates on every keystroke, and a proof you sketch on iPad lands in your source as LaTeX you can edit.

What's different

An engine, not a wrapper around one.

No TeX distribution runs behind the scenes. FlashTeX tokenizes, expands macros and lays out boxes and glue itself, which is why it can be fast and precise at the same time.

Preview

Every keystroke, not every save

Edits reuse the previous layout instead of recompiling the whole document, so a typical change renders in milliseconds.

Navigation

Click the page, land in the source

Every glyph in the preview knows the exact bytes it came from. Click a word or a symbol and the cursor goes straight there.

Capture

Handwriting becomes LaTeX

Draw with Pencil on iPad or photograph a page of notes. Grok converts it, and you review the result before it's inserted where you pinned it.

Recovery

One typo doesn't blank the page

A missing } or an unknown command produces a precise diagnostic, and the rest of the document keeps rendering around it.

Diagnostics

Errors that point at the exact character.

Each diagnostic carries its source range and says how the compiler recovered, so you fix the problem in place instead of hunting through a log.
