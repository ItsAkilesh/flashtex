\section*{Commutative diagrams with amscd}

The square commutes when $h\alpha=\beta f$ and $k\beta=\gamma g$:
\[
\begin{CD}
  A @>{f}>> B @>{g}>> C \\
  @V{\alpha}VV @V{\beta}VV @VV{\gamma}V \\
  D @>>{h}> E @>>{k}> F
\end{CD}
\]
The same arrow grid includes equality, a dashed label, and opposing vertical arrows:
\[
\begin{CD}
  X @= Y @>{u}>> Z \\
  @AAA @V{\scriptstyle s}VV @VV{\scriptstyle t}V \\
  P @<<{v}< Q @<<{w}< R
\end{CD}
\]
