    sections (ad-hoc only, `spctl` rejects, no Developer Program membership
    available here).
  - Local-network permission prompt: documented as **not scriptable by OS
    design**, not merely an environment limitation — macOS blocks
    AppleScript/System Events from reading or clicking TCC consent dialogs
    even with Accessibility permission, specifically to prevent
    self-granted permissions. Documented exactly what a human needs to do
    instead (open `Edit > Nearby Companion…`, toggle "Advertise", confirm
    the system prompt and its text, confirm the listener starts, confirm
    `System Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network` lists FlashTeX),
    and flagged as an open, untested question whether a rebuild's new
    ad-hoc signature forces re-prompting (explicitly not claimed as
    verified either way).
